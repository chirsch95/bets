## Why

v2 over-projects strikeouts by a **growing** margin on settled data
(4/30 → 6/8, n=1,054 graded starts):

| month | mean(proj_v2 − actual) |
|---|---|
| 2026-04 (partial) | +0.10 K |
| 2026-05 | +0.28 K |
| 2026-06 | +0.49 K |

Consequences observed everywhere downstream: model-favored Overs hit 43.6%
vs Unders 50.0% in the edge band; the suggester's Overs hit 47.8% vs Unders
58.9% (n=275 legs). The all-time Platt calibration cannot track a drifting
bias (it averages over the whole sample), so calibrated probabilities in the
bettable band still ran ~7pp hot.

## What v2bc is

`proj_ks_v2bc = max(0.05, proj_ks_v2 − bias)` where `bias` is the mean
signed error (proj − actual) of v2 over the trailing **30 days** of settled
starts, recomputed every pipeline run, **zeroed when fewer than 100 graded
starts** exist in the window. No new factors, no routing, one number — the
cheapest possible fix for the observed failure.

v2bc gets its own Platt scaler (`MODELS` in calibration.py) once ≥30 graded
rows exist; until then `cal_p_over_v2bc` falls back to the raw Poisson prob.

Promote v2bc to the production bet-classification source (i.e.
`cal_edge_v2bc` replaces `cal_edge_v2` in pickEdge/isBettableFocus) only if,

1. **Calibration:** leave-one-day-out Brier score of calibrated v2bc beats

3. **No new pathology:** v2bc's Over/Under hit-rate split is narrower than

If the trailing bias shrinks toward 0 on its own (v2's adjustment layers
catching up, per the MODEL_CHECK_2026-06-08 narrative), v2bc converges to v2
and the comparison is a harmless no-op — that's the desired failure mode.
